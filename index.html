Featured case studySingle-cell + spatial transcriptomics

Vascular repair after myocardial infarction

How do injury-associated states in vessel-supporting cells change over time, and what does their spatial RNA signal add to the interpretation?

Reanalysis of published single-cell and spatial transcriptomic data, presented as a report for experimental collaborators and supported by a modular Nextflow workflow and tutorial.

  • Study-design and biological-replication audit
  • Cell-state, injury-comparison, and spatial analyses
  • Explicit limitations and a discriminating follow-up experiment
