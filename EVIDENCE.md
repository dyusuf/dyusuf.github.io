## Homepage selection — 15 September 2026

The four expertise sections carry `data-evidence` references to this inventory:
AI (P02, P03, P12), omics (P01, P10), software (P05–P08, P11), and training (P04, P07).
Project summaries have been reduced to names, links and brief context.
Detailed claims in this inventory remain source context, not additional claims
made by the short homepage. The docking study (P09) is deferred from the homepage;
its original publication remains in the full research record.

The page continues to describe AI development and evaluation without claiming
a calibrated judge or completed expert adjudication. The vascular link identifies
a case study; its methods, results and review history remain in the course.
BinfoCheck is explicitly labeled as an MVP in development; its evidence entry
distinguishes implemented infrastructure from the unfinished end-to-end product.

The portfolio hub summarizes these projects; their owning repositories and
publications remain authoritative for implementation and scientific detail.
The single publication-record link remains in the introduction; the redundant
publication row was removed. The contact area links to the candidate's verified
public LinkedIn profile.
